## Resources

### Other Biodiversity / Natural History Databases
<p> [Paleobiology Database (PBDB)](https://paleobiodb.org/navigator/): database for deep time fossil occurrences</p>
<p>[iDigBio](https://www.idigbio.org/): portal that provides data and images for millions of biological specimens</p>
<p>[Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)](https://www.gbif.org/): an international network and data infrastructure aimed at providing open access data about all types of life on Earth</p>
<p>[Plant Names Database](https://www.plantfossilnames.org/): repository for fossil plant names and diagnoses</p>
<p>[Inside Wood](https://insidewood.lib.ncsu.edu/search): database and search portal for anatomical descriptions and photographs of modern and fossil wood; includes a schema for describing wood</p>
<p>[Macrostrat](https://macrostrat.org/): platform for the aggregation and distribution of geological data relevant to the spatial and temporal distribution of sedimentary, igneous, and metamorphic rocks as well as data extracted from them</p>
<p>[U.S. Geologic Names Lexicon (Geolex)](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Geolex/search): a U.S. national compilation of names and descriptions of geologic units</p>

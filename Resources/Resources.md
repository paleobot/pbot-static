## Resources

### Other Databases
[Paleobiology Database (PBDB)](https://paleobiodb.org/navigator/): database for deep time fossil occurrences
[iDigBio](https://www.idigbio.org/): portal that provides data and images for millions of biological specimens
[Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)](https://www.gbif.org/): an international network and data infrastructure aimed at providing open access data about all types of life on Earth
[Plant Names Database](https://www.plantfossilnames.org/): repository for fossil plant names and diagnoses
[Inside Wood](https://insidewood.lib.ncsu.edu/search): database and search portal for anatomical descriptions and photographs of modern and fossil wood; includes a schema for describing wood
[Macrostrat](https://macrostrat.org/): platform for the aggregation and distribution of geological data relevant to the spatial and temporal distribution of sedimentary, igneous, and metamorphic rocks as well as data extracted from them
[U.S. Geologic Names Lexicon (Geolex)](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Geolex/search): a U.S. national compilation of names and descriptions of geologic units

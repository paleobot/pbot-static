## Resources

### Other Biodiversity / Natural History Databases
[Paleobiology Database (PBDB)](https://paleobiodb.org/navigator/): database for deep time fossil occurrences</p>
[iDigBio](https://www.idigbio.org/): portal that provides data and images for millions of biological specimens</p>
[Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)](https://www.gbif.org/): an international network and data infrastructure aimed at providing open access data about all types of life on Earth</p>
[Plant Names Database](https://www.plantfossilnames.org/): repository for fossil plant names and diagnoses</p>
[Inside Wood](https://insidewood.lib.ncsu.edu/search): database and search portal for anatomical descriptions and photographs of modern and fossil wood; includes a schema for describing wood</p>
[Macrostrat](https://macrostrat.org/): platform for the aggregation and distribution of geological data relevant to the spatial and temporal distribution of sedimentary, igneous, and metamorphic rocks as well as data extracted from them</p>
[U.S. Geologic Names Lexicon (Geolex)](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Geolex/search): a U.S. national compilation of names and descriptions of geologic units</p>

### Image Repository Options
<p>As we do not have unlimited storage space, we strongly encourage you to include stable links to specimen images rather than uploading images here. Most museums and universities have digital repositories that generate stable links to individual specimen images. If you do not belong to an institution that has its own digital repository, here are some options that are PBot-compatible and free or low cost.</p>
[Internet Archive](https://archive.org/)</p>
[Morphosource](https://www.morphosource.org/)</p>
[FigShare](https://figshare.com/)</p>
[Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/)</p>
